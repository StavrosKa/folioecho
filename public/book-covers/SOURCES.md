# Real book cover sources

Gemini suggested edition ISBNs; title/author records were independently checked against Open Library. Incorrect ISBN suggestions were not trusted. These are reproductions of published covers, not AI illustrations or public-domain artwork. Rights remain with the respective publishers/artists.

Images are cached as small WebP files in `/book-covers/`; browsing makes no model calls or external image requests. Editions may differ from the books in a shop.
